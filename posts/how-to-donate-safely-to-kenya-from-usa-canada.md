  ## 🚩 Red Flags to Watch For
  
  Before donating to ANY international charity, watch for these warning signs:
  
  ### ❌ AVOID charities that:
  1. 🚨 **Pressure you to donate immediately** - "Act now or this family dies!"
  2. 💳 **Only accept untraceable payment methods** - Cash, gift cards, cryptocurrency ONLY
  3. 🏢 **Have no verifiable contact information** - No physical address, phone, or email
  4. 🧾 **Refuse to provide tax receipts** - Legitimate nonprofits ALWAYS provide receipts
  5. 🌐 **Have no online presence** - No website, social media, or reviews
  6. 😢 **Use emotional manipulation excessively** - Graphic suffering images designed to shock
  7. ❓ **Can't explain where money goes** - Vague statements about "helping people"
  8. ✍️ **Have spelling/grammar errors on website** - Sign of unprofessional or scam operation
  
  ### ⚠️ Real Scam Example
  In 2023, a fake "Kenya Children's Fund" stole $200,000 from USA donors using stolen photos of real children. The website looked legitimate but had no registered nonprofit status.

  ## 🔎 How to Verify a Legitimate Kenya Charity
  
  Follow this 7-step verification checklist:
  
  ### 🧾 Step 1: Check Nonprofit Registration
  **For USA Donors:**
  - Search GuideStar.org for 501(c)(3) status
  - Verify on IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search
  - Check Charity Navigator rating (aim for 3+ stars)
  
  **For Canadian Donors:**
  - Search Canada Revenue Agency Charities Listing
  - Check Charity Intelligence Canada
  
  ### 🔍 Step 2: Research the Organization
  - **Google the charity name + "scam" or "review"**
  - Check Better Business Bureau (BBB)
  - Read reviews on GreatNonprofits.org
  - Look for news articles or media coverage
  
  ### 📞 Step 3: Verify Contact Information
  Legitimate charities have:
  - 📍 Physical address (even if in Kenya)
  - ☎️ Working phone number
  - ✉️ Professional email (not free Gmail/Yahoo)
  - 📱 Active social media with regular updates
  - 🖼️ Real photos of operations (not stock images)
  
  ### 📊 Step 4: Check Financial Transparency
  Ask these questions:
  - ❓ "What percentage of donations goes directly to beneficiaries?"
  - 🧾 "Can I see your annual financial report?"
  - 💵 "How much does your CEO/director earn?" (Red flag if >$150K for small charity)
  - 🎯 "Where exactly will my donation be used?"
  
  **📊 Industry Standards:**
  - GOOD: 75%+ goes to programs
  - CONCERNING: 60-74% to programs
  - AVOID: <60% to programs
  
  ### ⏱️ Step 5: Test Their Responsiveness
  - Send an email with specific questions
  - Call their phone number
  - Comment on social media
  - **Legitimate organizations respond within 24-48 hours**
  
  ### 📈 Step 6: Review Their Impact Reports
  Look for:
  - Photos with dates and locations
  - Specific numbers (e.g., "Provided 47 wheelchairs in 2023")
  - Before/after documentation
  - Beneficiary testimonials
  - Regular updates (at least quarterly)
  
  ### 🔐 Step 7: Check Payment Security
  - Website must have SSL certificate (https://)
  - Should offer recognized payment processors (PayPal, Stripe)
  - Never provide bank login credentials
  - Avoid wire transfers to individuals

  ## 🧾 Tax Deductions for USA & Canada Donors
  
  ### 🇺🇸 USA Donors (IRS Guidelines)
  
  To claim your donation as tax-deductible:
  
  **✅ Requirements:**
  1. Charity must have 501(c)(3) status
  2. Keep receipt showing: charity name, date, amount, description
  3. For donations >$250, need written acknowledgment
  4. For non-cash donations >$500, file Form 8283
  
  **📈 Deduction Limits (2024):**
  - Cash donations: Up to 60% of AGI (Adjusted Gross Income)
  - Non-cash donations: Up to 50% of AGI
  - Cryptocurrency: Up to 30% of AGI (if held >1 year)
  
  **🧮 Example Tax Savings:**
  - Donate $1,000 to AbleHearts
  - Your tax bracket: 24%
  - Tax savings: $240
  - Effective cost: $760
  
  ### 🇨🇦 Canadian Donors (CRA Guidelines)
  
  **📈 Tax Credit Rates (2024):**
  - First $200 donated: 15% federal + provincial rate
  - Over $200 donated: 29% federal + provincial rate
  
  **🧮 Example (Ontario resident):**
  - Donate $1,000 to AbleHearts
  - First $200: 20.05% credit = $40
  - Remaining $800: 46.41% credit = $371
  - Total tax credit: $411
