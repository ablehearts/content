# Mary & Lydia's Story: 40+ Years of Survival Against All Odds

**⚠️ URGENT: This is not just a story of disability. This is a story of survival.**

Mary has lived with her disability for **40+ years**. Lydia for **20+ years**. But their greatest threats are not their disabilities—it's the land-grabbing authorities, suspected poisonings, untreated medical emergencies, and a community that wants them gone.

**They need permanent safe housing and urgent medical evacuation NOW.**

## 💪 Born Able-Bodied: Disabilities That Appeared Without Explanation

**Both Mary and Lydia were born NORMAL—fully able-bodied with no disabilities.**

Their disabilities developed mysteriously over time, transforming in "unexplainable ways" that doctors could not diagnose or reverse. What started as minor symptoms gradually escalated into permanent, life-altering mobility impairments.

- **Mary:** Disability onset 40+ years ago (cause unknown, irreversible)
- **Lydia:** Disability onset 20+ years ago (progressive deterioration, unexplained)

**Neither was born disabled. Life took that from them without warning and without cure.**

Despite decades of struggle, they have maintained hope and dignity—but now face threats far worse than their disabilities: **persecution, poisoning, and medical neglect**.

## 👩 Mary's Journey: 40+ Years of Disability + Recent Family Poisonings

Mary has lived with her disability for **over 40 years**. But in 2019, her life became a nightmare.

### ☠️ The 2019 Family Tragedy: Poisoning by In-Law Relative

**In 2019, Mary lost BOTH her mother and her sister within a 2-week span after they were poisoned at home.**

**What happened:**
- 🥤 **Mary's mother and sister drank water together at their home**
- 👥 **The water was served by a "close" in-law relative**
- ☠️ **Within hours, both became violently ill** ("hell broke loose")
- 🏥 **Rushed to hospital, but could not be saved**
- 💔 **Both health deteriorated rapidly**
- ⚰️ **Both died in the same hospital, 2 weeks apart**
- 😢 **Mary herself was a patient-looker at the hospital** - despite her own disability, she stayed by their bedsides with other supporting siblings, watching them die

**The suspected perpetrators and their motives:**
- 👥 **Close in-law relative** who served the poisoned water
- 🏘️ **Jealous family members** seeking to grab the land
- 🏘️ **Outsiders with land disputes** - neighbors coveting their property
- ⛪ **The neighboring church** - wants their land to build a house for their priest
- 👮 **The area chief** - complicit in the land-grabbing schemes

**The motive?** Senseless jealousy toward a family that has "nothing to envy"—two disabled women with no income, living in poverty, yet targeted for a small plot of land.

**Mary's words:** *"If my able-bodied mother and sister could be poisoned and killed, what protection do Lydia and I have as disabled, vulnerable women? She knows Lydia and her could be next."*

### 📅 Mary's Current Crisis (2024-2025)
- ♿ **40+ years of disability** with limited access to proper care
- 💔 **5+ years of trauma** from family poisonings (mother + sister, 2019)
- 🏚️ **EVICTION THREAT** - local church and chief want their ancestral land
- 🩸 **Diabetes worsening** due to chronic stress from threats
- 💔 **High blood pressure** requiring emergency hospital visits
- 😰 **Living in constant fear** - "We could be poisoned next"
- 💸 **Zero income** - cannot afford to relocate to safety
- 🙍‍♀️ **Social isolation** - community complicit in persecution

### 🆘 What Mary URGENTLY Needs:
1. **Permanent safe housing AWAY from current location** (land purchase + construction)
2. Ongoing medical care for diabetes and blood pressure
3. Legal protection from land-grabbing authorities
4. Psychological support for trauma from family deaths

**Shame on the church. Shame on the area chief. Disabled women are being persecuted for land.**

Lydia has lived with her disability for **over 20 years**. But her condition is deteriorating rapidly due to **complete medical neglect**.

### 🩹 The Untreated Wound Crisis (10+ YEARS)

**Lydia has open leg wounds that have gone untreated for MORE THAN 10 YEARS.**

**Current reality:**
- 🦟 **Wounds infested with insects** - she uses pieces of cloth to keep flies away
- 🤢 **Stench from rotting flesh** - wounds are festering and infected
- 🩹 **NO proper medical dressing** - only torn cloth strips
- 🙏 **Last professional wound care: 2023** - one-time treatment from a kind well-wisher who took her to have wounds medically cleaned and dressed
- 💔 **Since 2023:** No follow-up care, wounds have deteriorated back to crisis levels
- ⚠️ **Risk of sepsis, gangrene, amputation** - without urgent intervention, she will lose her legs or die
- 😢 **Constant pain** - sleeps with wounds exposed, cannot afford bandages

**She urgently needs MEDICAL EVACUATION to specialized facilities outside Kenya for:**
1. **Wound debridement** (surgical cleaning of dead tissue)
2. **Antibiotic treatment** (intravenous, hospital-grade)
3. **Skin grafts** if tissue is too damaged
4. **Long-term wound care** with proper dressings

**Without urgent airlifting to better medical facilities abroad, Lydia will die from infection.**

### 🦴 Jaw Bone Deterioration - Losing Ability to Speak and Eat

**Lydia's jaw bones are CLOSING**, making her unable to:
- 🗣️ **Speak clearly** - talking is becoming increasingly difficult
- 🍽️ **Eat solid food** - can only consume soft/liquid meals
- 😔 **Communicate her pain** - progressively losing her voice

**She needs:**
- 🏥 **Maxillofacial surgery** (jaw reconstruction)
- 🦴 **Bone grafts or implants** to prevent complete jaw closure
- 🩺 **Specialized dental/surgical care** (unavailable in rural Kenya)

## 🧱 Daily Challenges They Face

Their journey illustrates the systemic barriers faced by disabled individuals in Kenya:

### 🏥 Healthcare Access
- 🚑 **Limited availability** of specialized medical services
- 💊 **High costs** of medications and treatments
- 🚌 **Transportation barriers** to clinics and hospitals
- ♿ **Lack of assistive devices** like wheelchairs and mobility aids

### 🏗️ Infrastructure Barriers
- 🛗 Most buildings lack wheelchair ramps
- 🚌 Public transportation is inaccessible
- 🛣️ Roads and pathways are poorly maintained
- 🚻 Sanitation facilities are not adapted

### 💸 Economic Challenges
- 📉 **High unemployment** among disabled individuals (80%+ in Kenya)
- 🚫 **Discrimination** in hiring practices
- 🛠️ **Limited vocational training** opportunities
- 🏛️ **Inadequate government support** programs

### 🧠 Social Stigma
- ❌ Misconceptions about disability
- 🚪 Social exclusion from community activities
- 🗳️ Limited representation in decision-making
- 😔 Psychological impact of discrimination
