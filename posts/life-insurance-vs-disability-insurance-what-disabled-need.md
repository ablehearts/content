# Life Insurance vs Disability Insurance: What Disabled People Really Need

When you think about insurance, you probably think about protecting your family *after* you're gone. Life insurance. Death benefits. Financial security for your loved ones.

**But what about protection *while* you're still alive?** 🤔

For Mary and Lydia—two disabled women in rural Kenya struggling with mobility impairments and daily survival—the question isn't "What happens when we die?" It's **"How do we live with dignity today?"** 🦽

This is the crucial difference between **life insurance** and **disability insurance**. And for millions of disabled people worldwide, understanding this difference could mean the gap between despair and hope.

---

## ⚖️ The Fundamental Difference

### Life Insurance: Protection After Death

**Life insurance pays out when you die.** It's designed to:

- 💵 Replace lost income for surviving family members
- 🏠 Pay off debts (mortgages, loans)
- ⚰️ Cover funeral expenses
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Provide inheritance for children
- 🛡️ Ensure financial stability for dependents

**Who it helps:** Your family and beneficiaries *after* you're gone.

**Who it doesn't help:** You, while you're still alive and struggling.

---

### 💪 Disability Insurance: Protection While Living

**Disability insurance pays out when you become disabled and can't work.** It's designed to:

- 💰 Replace lost income during disability
- 🏥 Cover medical expenses
- 🦽 Pay for assistive devices (wheelchairs, prosthetics, hearing aids)
- 🏡 Fund home modifications (ramps, accessible bathrooms)
- 🍞 Support daily living expenses when you can't earn

**Who it helps:** You and your family *while you're alive* and need support.

**Who it doesn't help:** Your family after you die (that's life insurance's job).

---

## 🌍 The Global Insurance Gap

### Developed Countries vs. Developing Countries

**In wealthy nations:** 🇺🇸 🇬🇧 🇨🇦 🇦🇺
- ✅ Disability insurance is common (often through employers)
- ✅ Government disability benefits exist (Social Security Disability in the US 🇺🇸, Disability Living Allowance in the UK 🇬🇧)
- ✅ Healthcare coverage includes assistive devices
- ✅ Legal protections for disabled workers

**In developing countries like Kenya:** 🇰🇪
- ❌ Disability insurance is virtually nonexistent for low-income people
- ❌ Government support is minimal or inaccessible
- ❌ Healthcare is pay-out-of-pocket
- ❌ No employer benefits for informal workers
- ❌ No safety net for disabled rural residents

**The result?** Disabled people in developing countries face a double burden:
1. 🤕 The disability itself
2. 🚫 Complete lack of financial protection

## 🚧 The Problem: Disability Insurance Doesn't Exist for Them

### Why Poor, Disabled People Can't Get Coverage

**Barriers to disability insurance in rural Kenya:**

1. **💼 No formal employment** - Most insurance is employer-based; Mary & Lydia have no jobs
2. **💸 No income to pay premiums** - Insurance requires monthly payments they can't afford
3. **🏥 Pre-existing conditions** - Their disabilities existed before insurance, so they'd be excluded
4. **🏚️ No insurance infrastructure** - Private disability insurance barely exists in rural areas
5. **❓ Lack of awareness** - Many people don't even know disability insurance exists

**The vicious cycle:** 🔄
- Too poor to afford insurance
- Disability prevents earning income
- No income means no insurance
- No insurance means no support
- Poverty deepens, disability worsens

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can't they get government disability benefits in Kenya? 🇰🇪

**A:** Kenya has a National Council for Persons with Disabilities, but:
- ⚠️ Benefits are extremely limited
- 📋 Registration is difficult, especially in rural areas
- 💵 Monthly stipends (if available) are around $20-$50/month—nowhere near enough to survive
- ❌ Many disabled people never receive anything

Government support exists on paper but fails in practice.

### Q: Why don't they buy their own disability insurance? 💳

**A:** They can't afford it. Disability insurance premiums require:
- 💰 Regular income (which they don't have)
- 💼 Formal employment (which they don't have)
- 💸 Upfront payments (which they can't make)

Additionally, most insurers exclude pre-existing conditions, so their disabilities would disqualify them.

### Q: Is life insurance completely useless for disabled people? 🪦

**A:** Not completely, but it's a lower priority. Life insurance helps *after* death, but disabled people need support *during* life. If they had resources for both, great—but when resources are limited, **disability support comes first**. ✅

### Q: How much would disability insurance cost for someone like Mary or Lydia? 💵

**A:** In developed countries, disability insurance typically costs 1-3% of your income. For someone earning $30,000/year, that's $300-$900/year.

**But Mary and Lydia earn essentially $0/year.** 📉 So even "affordable" insurance is out of reach.
