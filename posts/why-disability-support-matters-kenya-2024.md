# Why Disability Support Matters in Kenya: Facts, Challenges & How to Help

  Millions of Kenyans live with disabilities, facing daily challenges that most of us never consider. Here’s the bigger picture—and how you can help.

  ## 🇰🇪 The Disability Landscape in Kenya

  - 👥 Over 3 million Kenyans live with disabilities
  - ♿ Limited access to assistive devices and mobility aids
  - 🏥 Gaps in affordable, accessible healthcare
  - 🏫 Barriers to inclusive education
  - 💼 High unemployment and economic exclusion

  ## 📊 Key Statistics

  - ♿ 60% lack access to assistive devices like wheelchairs
  - 📉 80%+ unemployment among disabled adults in many regions
  - 🏫 Thousands of children excluded due to inaccessible schools
  - 🏗️ Public infrastructure often lacks ramps and accessible facilities

  ## 🏗️ Systemic Challenges

  Infrastructure, social stigma, and economic barriers create a perfect storm of difficulty for disabled Kenyans:

  - 🚧 Inaccessible buildings and transport
  - 🧠 Social stigma and discrimination
  - 💸 Poverty cycles reinforced by limited opportunities
  - 🏛️ Insufficient government funding and services

  ## 🤝 Your Impact: What Donations Can Do

  - ♿ Provide durable wheelchairs and mobility aids
  - 🏥 Fund medical care, therapy, and medications
  - 🧑‍🏫 Support inclusive education and skills training
  - 🏠 Improve accessibility for safe, dignified living

  ## 🌟 Real Change Starts With You

  Together, we can turn systemic barriers into bridges of opportunity. Even modest monthly support can transform day-to-day life for someone living with a disability.
