**Accepted from:**
- ✅ India, Singapore, Japan, China, Philippines, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Hong Kong, South Korea

**How to donate:**
1. Go to: [/donate](/donate)
2. Click "Donate with PayPal"
3. Choose amount in your currency
4. PayPal auto-converts to USD/KES
5. Receive instant receipt

**Fees:**
- India: No foreign transaction fee (PayPal India)
- Singapore: Small conversion fee (~3%)
- Japan: 4% foreign transaction fee
- China: Limited availability (use credit card)

---

#### 💳 **2. Credit/Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX)**
**Accepted worldwide via Stripe**

**How to donate:**
1. Select "Credit/Debit Card" on donate page
2. Enter card details (secure Stripe processing)
3. Amount charged in USD
4. Your bank converts currency
5. Receive instant receipt

**Currency Conversion:**
- India: Check with your bank for forex rates
- Singapore: Usually 1-2% markup
- Japan: 2-3% forex fee
- International cards recommended

### **Why Asian Donors Connect with Their Story**

Mary (disabled mother) and Lydia (disabled daughter) live in rural Kenya with severe mobility challenges. After losing their primary caregiver in 2019, they survive on less than $3/day—similar to poverty levels in rural Asia.

**Before International Donors:**
- 😢 No wheelchair (Mary crawled for hours)
- 🏥 Untreated wounds (couldn't afford ₹1,000 INR equivalent)
- 🏚️ Leaking roof
- 📚 Lydia couldn't attend school (fees = ₹15,000 INR/year)

**After Asian Donors Helped:**
- ♿ Indian donor: Sponsored wheelchair (₹40,000 INR)
- 🏥 Singaporean donor: Monthly medical care ($50 SGD/month)
- 📚 Japanese donor: School fees for Lydia (¥20,000 JPY/year)

**Your donation continues their transformation.**
