## What is the Rat Race? 🐀🏃💨

**The rat race is a vicious cycle where you work to survive, borrow to live, and die in debt.** ⚰️💸

It looks like this: 📋

1. **You're told**: "Study hard, get good grades, you'll succeed" 🎓✨
2. **Reality**: You graduate with loans, no job, and crushing debt 💔📜
3. **You borrow** to eat, pay rent, dress presentably for interviews 🍽️🏠👔
4. **You borrow again** to pay the first loan 🔄💰
5. **Repeat** until you die ♾️⚰️

**It's called a "rat race" because:** 🐀
- You're running as fast as you can 🏃‍♂️💨
- You're going nowhere 🔄
- You're trapped in a cage (debt, bills, survival) 🗝️❌
- The only finish line is death 💀

And for **disabled people**, this race is **twice as hard** because: ♿💔
- They start with a handicap (literally) 🚧
- The track is not accessible 🚫
- No one gives them loans for wheelchairs or assistive devices 💸❌
- Employment doors are locked before they even knock 🚪🔒

## The Education Trap: HELB Loans & False Promises 🎓💸

### The Promise 🎯

> Education is the key to success 🔑. Get a degree, and your life will change. 📜✨

**The Reality in Kenya:** 🇰🇪

- **HELB (Higher Education Loans Board)** gives students loans to study 💰📚
- Students graduate with **Ksh 200,000 - 500,000+ in debt** 💸
- **No jobs** waiting for them 😰🚫
- **Loan repayment starts immediately** (whether you have income or not) ⏰💔
- **Interest accumulates** every month you can't pay 📈😱

### The Cruel Math 🔢💔

**Example:**
- HELB Loan: **Ksh 400,000** 💰
- Interest Rate: **4% per year** (compounding) 📊
- Repayment Period: **4 years after graduation** ⏳
- Monthly Payment Required: **~Ksh 9,000** 💸

**But:** 😰
- **60% of graduates** can't find employment in their field 🚫💼
- **Entry-level salary** (if you're lucky): Ksh 25,000/month 💵
- **After HELB payment**: Ksh 16,000 left 💸
- **Rent** (Nairobi): Ksh 10,000 minimum 🏠
- **Transport**: Ksh 3,000 🚌
- **Food**: Ksh 5,000 minimum 🍽️
- **What's left?** **-Ksh 2,000** (you're already in the negative!) ❌💔

**You're broke BEFORE you even live.** 😭💸

### Community Fundraisings (Harambees) 🤝💰

Many families hold **harambees** (community fundraisings) to send students to university:
- Relatives contribute Ksh 5,000, 10,000, 50,000 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦💵
- Parents sell livestock, land, or take high-interest loans 🐄🏡💸
- The student graduates... 🎓
- **And can't find a job** 😰🚫
- **And can't pay back the community** 💔
- **Shame, guilt, and family pressure** multiply 😞😣

**The education trap doesn't end at graduation—it follows you for life.** ♾️🪤

## The Employment Crisis: Degrees Without Jobs 🎓❌💼

### The Brutal Statistics 📊😱

In Kenya: 🇰🇪
- **1,000,000+ graduates** per year (universities + colleges) 🎓
- **~200,000 formal jobs** created per year 💼
- **800,000 graduates** with no employment 😰❌
- **Youth unemployment**: ~40% 📈💔

**What this means:** 😔
- 4 out of 5 graduates have NO job in their field 🚫
- Degrees in Engineering, Business, IT, Medicine—**useless without employment** 📜❌
- You spent 4 years studying, borrowed hundreds of thousands, and now you're **unemployed** 😭💸

### The Underemployment Trap 🔗💼

Even those "lucky enough" to find work face:
- **Underpayment**: Ksh 15,000 - 20,000 for graduates (less than minimum wage) 💸😭
- **No contracts**: Fired anytime, no benefits 📄❌
- **No job security**: "Probation" periods that last forever ⏳😰
- **Exploitation**: Expected to work 10+ hours, weekends, no overtime pay 😓⏰

**You're employed but still broke.** 💼💸😔

## The Borrowing Cycle: Rob Peter to Pay Paul 🔄💰

When you can't make ends meet, you start borrowing: 💸

### The Vicious Cycle 🔄😰

**Month 1:** 📅
- Borrow **Ksh 5,000** from Bob (friend) to pay rent 👨💰

**Month 2:** 📅
- Borrow **Ksh 7,000** from Paul (relative) to pay Bob back + buy food 👴💸🍽️

**Month 3:** 📅
- Borrow **Ksh 10,000** from John (mobile loan app) to pay Paul back + pay rent 📱💰
- **Interest**: 20% = you now owe John **Ksh 12,000** 📈😱

**Month 4:** 📅
- Borrow **Ksh 15,000** from Jane (chama/savings group) to pay John 👩💵
- Still need to eat, pay rent, transport... 🍽️🏠🚌

**Month 5:** 📅
- Borrow **Ksh 20,000** from digital lenders (Tala, Branch, M-Shwari) 📱💸
- **Interest**: 15-20% 📊
- **Now owe**: Ksh 24,000 💔

**Month 6:** 📅
- Can't pay anyone 😭❌
- CRB (Credit Reference Bureau) listing 📋🚫
- No more loans available 💸❌
- Relatives stop picking up your calls 📞🔇
- Friends avoid you 👥➡️👻

**You're trapped.** 🔒💔

### The Mobile Loan Trap

Digital lending apps in Kenya:
- **Tala, Branch, M-Shwari, Fuliza, KCB M-PESA...**
- **Easy to get**: Instant approval
- **Deadly interest**: 15-20% per month (not per year!)
- **CRB blacklisting**: Miss one payment, you're blocked from all formal credit
- **Harassment**: Daily calls, SMS, threats

**A Ksh 5,000 loan becomes Ksh 8,000 in 3 months if unpaid.**

You borrow to survive. You can't repay. You borrow again. **The cycle never ends.**

## Taxation: Death by a Thousand Cuts

As if low wages and high costs weren't enough, the government keeps **inventing new taxes**:

### Taxes in Kenya (Growing List)

1. **Income Tax (PAYE)**: 10-30% of your salary
2. **VAT**: 16% on almost everything
3. **Housing Levy**: 1.5% of gross salary (mandatory)
4. **NHIF**: Ksh 500 - 1,700/month (healthcare)
5. **NSSF**: 6% of salary (pension)
6. **Fuel Levy**: Increases transport and food costs
7. **Digital Services Tax**: 1.5% on online services
8. **Import Duty**: Makes everything expensive
9. **Excise Duty**: On alcohol, cigarettes, even bottled water
10. **County Taxes**: Business permits, cess, parking...

**You earn Ksh 30,000.**
**After taxes:** ~Ksh 21,000
**After rent, transport, food:** ~Ksh 2,000 left

**And they're planning more taxes:**
- Motor vehicle tax
- Eco-levy on plastics
- Social health insurance (replacing NHIF)

**Every new tax announcement = less money in your pocket.**

## Even Professionals Are Suffering

### Doctors, Teachers, Engineers—All Struggling

**Kenyan doctors** (2024 strike):
- Internship stipend: **Ksh 70,000/month**
- After 7 years of medical school
- After Ksh 500,000+ in loans
- **Government owes them 6+ months of unpaid salaries**
- They went on **strike for 56 days**
- Some work in hospitals with **no water, no equipment, no drugs**

**Teachers** (perpetual strikes):
- Average salary: **Ksh 25,000 - 40,000/month**
- Expected to teach 40+ students per class
- Buy their own teaching materials
- Walk 5km+ to rural schools
- **Unpaid allowances for years**

**Engineers**:
- Degree cost: Ksh 600,000+ (4-5 years)
- Graduate to find: **No jobs**
- Or: Paid Ksh 20,000/month as "interns"
- Work for years with no contracts, no benefits

**Even the "successful" professionals are protesting unpaid and underpaid services.**

If doctors and teachers can't survive, **what hope do the rest of us have?**

## The Capitalist Scavenger System

### How Capitalism Feeds on You Until You Die

**Stage 1: Education (Ages 18-25)**
- Take HELB loans
- Take bank loans
- Family fundraising (harambees)
- **Debt: Ksh 400,000+**

**Stage 2: Job Hunting (Ages 22-30)**
- Spend money on:
  - CV printing, applications
  - Transport to interviews
  - Phone credit for follow-ups
  - "Smart" clothes for interviews
- Borrow for rent while jobless
- **Debt accumulates to Ksh 600,000+**

**Stage 3: Low-Wage Employment (Ages 25-35)**
- Finally get a job: Ksh 25,000/month
- Pay HELB: Ksh 9,000
- Pay rent: Ksh 10,000
- Pay mobile loans: Ksh 5,000
- **You're left with Ksh 1,000 for food, transport, life**
- Borrow more to survive

**Stage 4: Marriage & Family (Ages 28-40)**
- Get married (borrow for wedding)
- Have kids (borrow for hospital bills)
- School fees start (borrow for fees)
- **Debt: Ksh 1,500,000+**

**Stage 5: Middle Age (Ages 40-55)**
- Still paying HELB (penalties, interest)
- Still renting (can't afford land/house)
- Kids in university (more HELB loans for them)
- Health issues (borrow for treatment)
- **Debt: Ksh 3,000,000+**

**Stage 6: Old Age (Ages 55-70)**
- Retire with nothing
- NSSF pension: Ksh 5,000/month (if lucky)
- Still in debt
- Rely on children (who are also in debt)

**Stage 7: Death**
- Die in debt
- Family inherits debt
- Funeral fundraising (harambee)

**Capitalism scavenges your entire life—from birth to death.**

## For Disabled People: The Rat Race on Hard Mode

Now imagine running this rat race with **two legs broken** (literally).

### The Disabled Reality

**Education Challenges:**
- Most schools are **not accessible** (no ramps, no disabled toilets)
- Teachers have **no training** for disabled students
- Bullying, stigma, isolation
- Higher **dropout rates**

**HELB Loans:**
- Disabled students get **the same loan amounts** (no special consideration)
- But need **additional costs**: wheelchairs, assistive devices, medical care
- **No loans for wheelchairs** (Ksh 50,000 - 200,000)
- **No loans for prosthetics** (Ksh 100,000 - 500,000)

**Employment Crisis (Worse for Disabled):**
- **80%+ of disabled graduates** are unemployed
- Employers cite "health risks," "insurance costs," "accessibility issues"
- Even qualified disabled people are **rejected for their disability**
- **No workplace accommodations**

**The Borrowing Cycle:**
- Disabled people need to borrow **MORE** (for medical care, assistive devices)
- But get **LESS** (lenders see them as "high risk")
- **No one gives loans for wheelchairs or prosthetics**

**Inflation Hits Harder:**
- Assistive devices? Imported = high cost + inflation
- Medicine? Monthly expense
- Accessible housing? More expensive

**Taxation:**
- Same taxes as everyone else
- But **lower or no income**
- **No tax breaks for disability expenses**

**Even professionals who are disabled face:**
- Discrimination
- Inaccessible workplaces
- Lower pay (if hired at all)

**For disabled people, the rat race is not just hard—it's almost impossible to enter.**

Mary and Lydia face a **double whammy**: 😢💥

- **Living with disability** ♿
- Needs assistive devices: **Cost: Ksh 150,000+** 💰

- No loans available (no collateral, seen as "high risk") 🏦🚫

- **Can't work** (no accessible workplaces) 💼🚫

### Lydia's Reality 👧♿
- **Born with disabilities** 🎗️
- Needs prosthetics, wheelchairs, medical care: **Cost: Ksh 200,000+** 💰💊
- Same barriers as Mary 🚧
- Dependent on family (who are also broke) 👨‍👩‍👧💸

### The Family Reality 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦💔
- **Two disabled people in one household** ♿♿
- Medical costs: **Ksh 20,000+/month** 💊💰
- No income 💵❌
- No HELB loans (never went to university) 📚🚫
- No employment (can't access jobs) 💼❌
- No mobile loans (no income to repay) 📱💸❌
- **Complete exclusion from the rat race** 🐀🚫

**They're not even allowed to run—they're locked outside the cage.** 🔒💔

## Breaking the Cycle: What Needs to Change 🔨🔓

### Government & Policy Changes 🏛️📜

1. **Debt Forgiveness for Graduates** 🎓❌💸
   - Cancel HELB loans after 10 years of non-repayment ✂️
   - Base repayment on **income**, not fixed amounts 💰

2. **Universal Basic Income for Disabled People** ♿💵
   - Monthly stipend to cover basic needs 🏠🍽️
   - No conditions, no bureaucracy ✅

3. **Tax Reform** 💸📊
   - Stop inventing new taxes ✋❌
   - Tax the ultra-wealthy, not the poor 💰⚖️
   - Zero tax on basic food items 🍞🚫

4. **Employment Quotas** 💼✅
   - Mandate 5% of jobs for disabled people ♿💼
   - Penalties for non-compliance ⚠️

5. **Accessible Education** 📚♿
   - Free assistive devices for disabled students 🦽💊
   - Accessible schools, trained teachers 🏫👨‍🏫

### Individual & Community Actions 🤝💪

1. **Support Grassroots Charities** (like AbleHearts) 💝
   - Direct support to disabled individuals 👥💰
   - No middlemen, no bureaucracy ✅

2. **Hire Disabled People** 💼♿
   - If you own a business, give them a chance 🏢💪
   - Remote work is accessible 💻🏠

3. **Advocate for Policy Change** 📢✊
   - Sign petitions 📝
