---

## Meet Mary & Lydia 👩‍👧

**Mary** is a disabled mother. **Lydia** is her disabled daughter. Both share the Onchangu surname from Mary's father. Together, they face the daily challenges of living with disabilities in poverty, while caring for a young orphan girl who acts as their "legs" when they need help. 💔

---

## The Christmas Atmosphere Begins 🎅

It's November 6, 2025, and the atmosphere has already started shifting toward Christmas celebrations. 🎄✨

**For most people:**
- Shopping malls play festive music 🎵
- Decorations appear in stores and streets 🎀
- Families start planning reunions 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
- Excitement builds for the holidays 🎁

**But for Mary & Lydia:**
- Every day feels the same 😔
- No decorations, no celebrations 🚫🎄
- No family gatherings to look forward to 💔
- Just the same daily struggle for survival 😰

---

## When Everything Changed: 2019 🕊️

### The Loss That Changed Everything 💔

In 2019, **Mary's mother passed away**. She wasn't just a caregiver—she was **the unifying factor** that held the entire extended family together. 👵❤️

**What Mary's mother meant to the family:**

1. **Main Caregiver** 🏥
   - Took care of Mary & Lydia's daily needs
   - Managed their disabilities with love and patience
   - Ensured they never felt like a burden

2. **Family Anchor** ⚓
   - Brought all siblings together regularly
   - Organized family gatherings and celebrations
   - Resolved conflicts and maintained peace

3. **Grandchildren's Beloved Grandma** 👵💕
   - Sweet grandmother to all grandchildren and great-grandchildren
   - Created warm memories and traditions
   - Source of wisdom and unconditional love

### The 2019 Christmas That Never Felt the Same 🎄😢

That year, Christmas came just months after she died. It was the first Christmas without her.

**The impact was immediate:**
- Grandchildren and great-grandchildren felt lost without their sweet grandma 😭
- The family gathering felt incomplete 💔
- The joy was replaced with painful absence 🕊️
- Everyone realized: **she was the reason they came together** 👵✨

---

## The Family Bond Broken 💔

### What Happens When the Unifying Factor is Gone? 🧩

Mary's mother was the glue. Without her:

**Then (when Mary's mother was alive):**
- Regular family gatherings 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
- Mary's siblings stayed connected 🤝
- Mary & Lydia were cared for and visited often by family 💕
- Strong extended family bond ❤️

**Now (after 2019):**
- Family gatherings stopped ❌
- Most of Mary's siblings drifted apart 😔
- Only a few check on Mary & Lydia occasionally 📱
- The family bond is broken 💔

### Why Mary's Siblings Keep Away 🤔

Mary had siblings (Lydia's aunts and uncles) who used to stay connected when their mother was alive. But now most keep their distance.

**Some of Mary's siblings still check up:**
- A few visit occasionally to see how Mary & Lydia are doing ✅
- They care but life is hard for them too 😓

**But most of Mary's siblings keep away. Why?**

1. **Hardship of Their Own Lives** 💸
   - Struggling with their own families
   - Barely making ends meet
   - Can't afford to help financially

2. **The Invisible Sibling Rivalry** 👥
   - Funny how there exists both **love and hate** among siblings born to the same parents 🤝💔
   - As kids, the rivalry is weak and playful 👧👦
   - **As people get old, the hate becomes real** 😡
   - Some siblings become more successful than others 📈📉
   - **Success breeds jealousy and distance** 💰😠

*(Note: This isn't about greed—that's a topic for another post. But sibling dynamics are complex, especially when life outcomes differ dramatically.)* 🔄

## Mary & Lydia's Reality in 2025 😔

### Every Day Feels the Same ⏰

For people with disabilities living in poverty:

**No special seasons:**
- No weekend vs weekday ❌
- No holidays vs regular days ❌
- **Every day is just survival** 😰

**Daily challenges:**
- Managing disabilities without proper care 🏥
- Worrying about food and basic needs 🍞
- Caring for the orphan girl in their custody 👧
- No hope for the future 🌑

**The crushing loneliness:**
- Most family members don't visit anymore 😢
- Community support is limited 🚫
- Feeling forgotten and abandoned 💔

## The Meaning of the Festive Season 🎄✨

### For the Majority: A Time of Joy and Reflection 🎅

**Why this season matters to most people:**

1. **Rest and Rejuvenation** 😌
   - Break from work and stress
   - Time to recharge for the new year 🔋

2. **Leaving the "Concrete Jungle" 🏙️➡️🌳**
   - Many leave their jobs in the city
   - Return to local villages
   - Reconnect with the real world 🌍

3. **Family Reunions** 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
   - Visit home and check on changes
   - Some have left home to change home 🏡
   - This is the season to go back and check

4. **Reflection and Planning** 📔
   - Check the diary and mark achievements ✅
   - Reflect on inspirations 💭
   - Get fuel to work on the rest of the coming year ⛽

5. **The Moment for Everything Nice** 🎁✨
   - Celebration, joy, giving
   - Creating memories
   - Living in the moment 🎉

### The Festive Trap: Overspending and January Reality 💸😰

**The pattern many people follow:**

**December:**
- Overspend on unnecessary things 💸🎁
- Live in the moment without planning 🎉
- Enjoy the festivities fully 🥳

**January:**
- School fees are due 📚💰
- Money is gone 😱
- Hardship starts 😓
- Regret sets in 😢

**The clever approach:**
- **Separate enjoyment savings** into categories:
  - **Health** 🏥
  - **Education** 📚
  - **General expenditure** 💵
- Enjoy the festive season **without sacrificing the future** ✅

### For the Disabled Family: Just Live the Current Moment 😔

**Mary & Lydia's reality:**
- No money to split into categories ❌
- No savings for education, health, or future ❌
- **Just live the current moment and survive** 💔

**But with compassionate souls:**
- If people help boost them a bit 💝
- They can have savings ✅
- **Hardship has trained them to use resources well** 💪
- They won't waste it—they'll allocate wisely 📊

**Critical needs:**
- **School fees for the orphan girl** 👧📚
- Basic food and medical supplies 🍞🏥
- Dignity and hope for the future ✨

## Fun Fact: Mary the "Mtakatifu" 👼

### The Holy Mother 🙏

**Community nickname:**
- The community refers to Mary as **"mtakatifu"** (Swahili for "the holy") 🕊️
- They call her **"Mother of Jesus"** 🙏✨

**Why this nickname?**
- **She shares the name Mary** with the Mother of Jesus 🙏✨
- Her character resembles that holy nature:
  - Kindness and patience despite suffering 💕
  - Deep faith and resilience 🙏
  - The way she cares for the orphan girl like her own 👧❤️
- The name + the character = "Mtakatifu" (the holy one) 🕊️

**This festive season:**
- We can make **"Mother Mary"** even happier 😊🎄
- Show her that she's not forgotten 💝
- Bring joy to the "holy mother" 👼✨
