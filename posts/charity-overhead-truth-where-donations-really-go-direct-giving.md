You'll hear charity watchdogs say: *"Overhead doesn't matter - it's about impact!"*

**Translation:** *"Please don't look too closely at where your money goes."*

**The uncomfortable truth:**

### The Deceptive Accounting Tricks

Some charities use creative accounting to hide overhead:

1. **"Program expenses" that aren't really programs:**
   - Fundraising mailings labeled as "education"
   - Awareness campaigns that are really marketing
   - "Administrative" staff reclassified as "program" staff

2. **The 80/20 illusion:**
   - "80% goes to programs!" sounds great
   - But "programs" includes staff salaries, office rent for "program" teams
   - Actual cash/goods to beneficiaries? Often only 50-60%

3. **Delayed recognition:**
   - Donations this year spent on "infrastructure"
   - Impact (maybe) next year
   - Your $100 sits in their bank account earning them interest

## 💡 How to Evaluate Any Charity (Before Donating)

### The 5 Questions Every Philanthropist Should Ask

**1. What percentage goes to overhead?**
- ✅ Under 10%: Excellent
- ⚠️ 10-20%: Acceptable
- 🚨 20-30%: High (question if necessary)
- ❌ 30%+: Excessive (avoid)

**2. What does "program expenses" actually include?**
- Ask for a detailed breakdown
- Watch for inflated salaries labeled as "programs"

**3. Can I see proof of impact?**
- Photos with dates
- Receipts
- Testimonials from actual beneficiaries

**4. How long until my donation creates impact?**
- ✅ Days to weeks: Efficient
- ⚠️ Months: Bureaucratic
- 🚨 "We're building infrastructure": Your $ sits unused

**5. Do I know the person I'm helping?**
- ✅ Yes (name, face, story): True connection
- ❌ No (just "beneficiaries in Africa"): Impersonal, unverifiable

### Red Flags 🚩

- ❌ **Won't disclose overhead percentage**
- ❌ **CEO makes more than $200k**
- ❌ **Aggressive fundraising tactics** (guilt, urgency, emotional manipulation)
- ❌ **Vague impact reports** ("helped thousands!")
- ❌ **Can't provide receipts or photos**
- ❌ **No direct contact with beneficiaries**
