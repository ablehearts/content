## 🚩 Red Flags: Spotting Charity Scams

### ❌ IMMEDIATE RED FLAGS - Do NOT Donate If:

1. **🚨 High-pressure tactics**
   - "Mary will die without your donation TODAY!"
   - "Only 2 spots left for donors!"
   - Countdown timers on donation pages

2. **💳 Suspicious payment requests**
   - Wire transfers to individual accounts
   - Cash or gift cards only
   - Cryptocurrency ONLY (legitimate charities offer multiple options)
   - Payment to personal PayPal/Venmo accounts

3. **🏢 No verifiable organization**
   - No EIN (Employer Identification Number)
   - Can't find on GuideStar or IRS database
   - Gmail/Yahoo email addresses
   - No physical address or PO Box only

4. **🧾 Refusal to provide documentation**
   - "We don't do tax receipts"
   - Won't share financial reports
   - No proof of 501(c)(3) status
   - Vague answers about where money goes

## 🔎 7-Step Verification Process for USA Donors

Follow this checklist to verify ANY Kenya charity:

### Step 1: Verify 501(c)(3) Status ⭐ CRITICAL

**IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search:**
1. Go to irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search
2. Search charity name
3. Verify "Current" status
4. Note the EIN (Employer Identification Number)

**GuideStar.org:**
- Free Bronze profile = basic verification
- Gold/Platinum = excellent transparency
- Look for "Seal of Transparency"

### Step 2: Check Charity Watchdogs 🐕

**Charity Navigator** (charitynavigator.org)
- Rating: Aim for 3+ stars
- Score breakdown: Financial health, accountability, transparency
- CEO compensation listed

**Better Business Bureau** (give.org)
- Accredited charity seal
- Complaint history
- BBB rating (A+ to F)

### Step 3: Financial Transparency Check 💰

Ask for or search for:
- **Form 990** (required annual filing)
  - Shows revenue, expenses, CEO salary
  - Available on GuideStar or ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer
- **Annual Report** with breakdown:
  - Program expenses (should be 75%+)
  - Administrative costs (should be <15%)
  - Fundraising costs (should be <10%)

## 🧾 Maximizing Your Tax Deduction (IRS Guidelines 2025)

### Am I Eligible for a Tax Deduction?

**✅ Yes, if:**
- Charity has 501(c)(3) status
- You itemize deductions (not standard deduction)
- You have a receipt

**❌ No, if:**
- Charity is foreign without USA 501(c)(3) partnership
- You take the standard deduction ($13,850 single, $27,700 married)
- No documentation of donation

### How Much Can I Deduct?

**Cash Donations (2025 limits):**
- Up to **60% of your Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)**

**Cryptocurrency/Stock Donations:**
- Up to **30% of AGI** (if held >1 year)

### Calculate Your Tax Savings

**Example: $1,000 donation to AbleHearts**

If your tax bracket is **24%**:
- Tax savings: $1,000 × 24% = **$240**
- Net cost: $1,000 - $240 = **$760**
- You helped Mary & Lydia for $760, saved $240 on taxes!

If your tax bracket is **32%** (higher income):
- Tax savings: $1,000 × 32% = **$320**
- Net cost: **$680**

## ❓ FAQ: American Donors Ask

### Q: Will I get audited by the IRS for donating to Kenya?
**A:** No! Donating to a 501(c)(3) is completely normal. You only need to report foreign gifts >$15,797 (Form 3520). Donating to a registered charity (even if they operate in Kenya) is NOT a foreign gift.

### Q: Can I donate stock instead of cash?
**A:** Yes! Donating appreciated stock saves capital gains tax. You deduct fair market value and pay no capital gains on appreciation.
