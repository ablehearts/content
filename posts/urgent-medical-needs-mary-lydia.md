### For Mary
1. **Orthopedic Boot Shoes** - Custom-fitted medical footwear to support her mobility
2. **Ergonomic Wheelchair** - Adjustable wheelchair for daily activities and independence
3. **Medical Crutches** - Ergonomic crutches to assist with walking and balance

### For Lydia
1. **Standing Wheelchair** or **Ergonomic Wheelchair** - Therapeutic wheelchair that allows both sitting and standing positions
2. **Mobility Aids** - Additional support equipment as recommended by medical professionals

## Why These Items Are Critical

### Orthopedic Boot Shoes for Mary
- Provide proper foot support and alignment
- Reduce pain during daily activities
- Prevent further physical complications
- Enable safer movement around the home and community

### Ergonomic Wheelchair for Mary
- Offers adjustable seating for comfort during extended use
- Provides independence for daily tasks
- Reduces physical strain on caregivers
- Enables participation in community activities

### Standing Wheelchair for Lydia
- Therapeutic benefits of standing position
- Improves circulation and bone health
- Provides psychological benefits of eye-level interaction
- Reduces pressure sores from prolonged sitting
- Supports Lydia's remarkable cognitive abilities by enabling better participation

### Medical Crutches for Mary
- Ergonomic design reduces hand and wrist strain
- Adjustable height for proper posture
- Durable construction for long-term use
- Improves balance and confidence while walking

## The Current Situation

Mary and Lydia face daily challenges due to **lack of proper medical equipment**:

- **Limited Mobility**: Unable to move freely within and outside their home
- **Dependency**: Require constant assistance for basic activities
- **Health Risks**: Improper support leads to additional physical strain
- **Social Isolation**: Difficulty participating in community activities
- **Lost Opportunities**: Lydia's photographic memory and potential limited by mobility constraints

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why buy equipment instead of providing cash?**
A: Medical equipment is a **one-time investment** that provides long-term benefits. It empowers Mary & Lydia with independence and improved health for years to come.

**Q: Can I buy equipment locally in Kenya for them?**
A: Yes! If you're in Kenya or know local suppliers, contact us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/254107145882) to coordinate direct delivery.

**Q: How do I know the equipment will actually reach them?**
A: We provide photo/video proof of delivery and encourage donors to follow our [WhatsApp Channel](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbB81btJENy6GLqfBW0q) for real-time updates.

**Q: Can I donate used medical equipment?**
A: Absolutely! As long as it's in good working condition and suitable for their needs. Contact us to arrange shipping logistics.

**Q: What happens if you receive more donations than needed for equipment?**
A: Excess funds go toward:
1. Equipment maintenance and repairs
2. Medical consultations and fittings
3. Monthly living expenses (food, housing, education)
4. Emergency medical care

All allocations are transparently reported.
