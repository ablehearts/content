## The Capitalism Question: Why Does It Thrive? 📈💵

### Capitalism = Controlled Greed 🏦⚖️

**The system**:
1. You want more money 💰
2. You work harder / innovate 💡
3. You get rewarded 🏆
4. Society benefits (sometimes) 🌍✨

**Examples**:
- **Elon Musk**: Wants to be richer → Builds Tesla, SpaceX → We get electric cars, reusable rockets 🚀⚡
- **Steve Jobs**: Wanted Apple to dominate → Created iPhone → We all have smartphones 📱💎
- **Pharmaceutical companies**: Want profit → Develop vaccines → Millions saved (but also: price gouging) 💊💰

**The paradox**: Greed drives innovation. But greed also corrupts. 🔄

## The Paperwork Question: Why Do We Need It? 📄🖊️

### Money = Paperwork 💵📜

**What is money?**
- Not gold (we left the gold standard) 🪙❌
- Not real (it's just numbers on a screen) 💻💰
- **Just paperwork we all agree has value** 📄🤝

**Why?**
- Without money, how do you trade? 🔄
- Farmer produces food 🌾
- Doctor provides medicine 💊
- How do they exchange? 🤷

**Barter system?** Too slow. ⏳
**Money?** Fast, efficient. ⚡💵

---

### Visas = Paperwork for Humans 🛂📄

**Why do we need visas to travel?** 🌍✈️

**The logic**:
- **Security**: Know who's entering 🛡️
- **Resources**: Prevent overwhelming systems 🏥🏫
- **Control**: Regulate population movement 📊

**But animals move freely. Why can't humans?** 🦅🌍

**The answer**: Because humans compete for resources. 🏠💰🔄

If everyone could move anywhere:
- Rich countries would be flooded 🌊🇺🇸🇬🇧
- Poor countries would empty out 🏜️🇸🇴
- Systems would collapse 💥

**Paperwork (visas) controls the flow.** 📄✅

---

### Exams = Paperwork for Knowledge 📝🎓

**Why do we need exams?** 🤔

**The logic**:
- **Prove you know something** ✅📚
- **Filter for jobs** 💼🎯
- **Standardize competence** 📊

**But why can't we just demonstrate skills?** 🛠️💡

**The problem**:
- **Time**: Can't test everyone individually ⏰
- **Fairness**: Need objective metrics 📏
- **Scale**: Millions of students, limited jobs 👥💼

**Exams are paperwork to prove your brain has value.** 🧠📄💵
